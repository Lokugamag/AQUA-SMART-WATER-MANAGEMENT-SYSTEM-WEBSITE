WHO ARE WE ?

Making Water Information More Useful

AQUA focuses on smart water management technologies and solutions. Our goal is to help customers explore suitable ways to measure, monitor and understand water usage.

Company Background

AQUA Smart is a smart water management solutions initiative focused on improving the way water resources are monitored, managed, and utilized through modern technology. By combining innovative water metering technologies with intelligent monitoring systems, AQUA Smart aims to provide reliable and efficient solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. Operating under ABC Trade & Investments, AQUA Smart focuses on delivering technology-driven solutions that support accurate water consumption monitoring, improved resource management, and greater operational visibility.
